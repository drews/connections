# Spore stages

Cell, Creature, Tribe, Civilization, Space. Each stage reuses the world you built in the last one, but the verbs change completely: you stop steering a cell and start herding a tribe.

The camera pulls back at each transition. That pull-back is the most emotional moment in the game, because it reframes everything you just did as a *component* of something larger.

- Stage = regime. The game doesn't let you micromanage cells from space.
- Your creations persist across scales, which is what makes zooming feel like inhabiting rather than browsing.
- Relates to [[Powers of Ten]] and [[Map is not the territory]].
