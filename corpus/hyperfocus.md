# Hyperfocus

Hyperfocus is fully zoomed in: one spark fills the whole screen and the rest of the cosmos disappears.

The cost is not the dive. It's the surfacing: coming back up and not knowing which territory you're standing in.

- Needs a [[Re-entry ramps|re-entry ramp]].
- The zoom-out key should always be one keystroke away, like holding your breath and kicking toward the light.
