# Noise fields

Simplex noise as a substrate for a terminal UI: a slowly drifting field of glyph densities under the graph.

- Monochrome; texture carries meaning instead of color.
- The field can be modulated: by lens, by recency, by agent activity.
- See [[Microscope lenses]], [[Breathing interfaces]].
