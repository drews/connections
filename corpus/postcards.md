# Agent postcards

Agents shouldn't stream logs at me. They should send postcards: a short message pinned to a place in the map.

- A postcard lives at a scale. Zoomed out, ten postcards in a territory are one warm glow with a count.
- I read them when I'm *there*, not when they arrive. No notifications.
