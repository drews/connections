# Powers of Ten

The Eames film moves the camera out by a factor of ten every ten seconds, from a picnic blanket to the edge of the observable universe, then back down into a carbon nucleus.

What makes it work is not the zoom itself but that every stop is a *complete world* with its own rules. The blanket is a social scene. The city is a grid. The galaxy is a spiral. Nothing at one scale explains the next.

- A second brain needs the same: stops where the regime changes, not a smooth blur of ever-smaller text.
