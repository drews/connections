# Zettelkasten

Luhmann's slip box: atomic notes, dense links, structure emerging from the links rather than from folders.

- Sparks are zettels below the note level.
- What it lacked was a way to *see* the emergent structure at any scale. See [[Second brain]], [[Capture without filing]].
