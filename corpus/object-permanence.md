# Object permanence

Out of sight, out of mind is literal for me. Notes I can't see stop existing.

A zoomable map helps because nothing is ever *filed away*; it's just further out. Zoom out far enough and everything is on screen again, as a glow.

- The cosmos view is an object-permanence prosthetic.
