# Semantic zoom

Geometric zoom makes things bigger. Semantic zoom makes things *different*: at a distance a city is a dot with a name, closer it becomes districts, closer still streets.

Level-of-detail in games is the same trick in service of frame rate; in a knowledge tool it is in service of attention.

- The trick is choosing what survives a zoom-out: a name, a count, a temperature.
- Pairs with [[Level of detail]] and [[Powers of Ten]].
