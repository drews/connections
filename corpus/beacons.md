# Beacons

A beacon is me telling the agents where I am, on purpose. "I'm in this constellation; the question is X."

- It is blurred up to each agent's aperture. An agent with territory aperture learns the territory, not the note.
- The opposite of surveillance: presence that is *declared*, not inferred.
