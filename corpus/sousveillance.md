# Sousveillance not surveillance

I want tools that let me see myself, without a vendor or a model also seeing me by default.

- Data stays in plain files I own.
- No keystroke capture, no activity feeds, no "productivity score".
- Agents get what I give them, at the resolution I choose. See [[The membrane]].
