# Capture without filing

Every "where should this go?" question costs a thought. Capture should cost zero decisions.

- Drop the thought wherever you are zoomed. It lands near what you were looking at.
- Let structure *emerge* from links and similarity rather than folders.
- The machine can propose a home later, via [[Agent postcards]].
